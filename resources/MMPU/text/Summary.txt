I haven't made a full Mega Man Powered Up series, but after doing the <a href="https://retroachievements.org/game/20229">RetroAchievements bonus set</a> for this game, I've learned a fair bit about how to play through this game damageless.  I'll certainly forget a lot of this info eventually, so I want to record it here in case it's useful to anybody else.  Much of this info comes from a chat with another person called GreninjaMan who did his own damageless runs of the game, so the topics are a bit scattered and the writing may seem a bit odd.  I don't have the time to devote to fleshing this out into a full guide at this point in time though.

If you want to see all the robot master boss strategies put into practice, I've included a <a href="https://lockirby2.neocities.org/resources/MMPU/#copy-with-oilman">video</a> of Copy Robot's stage damageless with Oilman.  The boss write-ups are all written from the perspective of fighting them with Oilman in Copy Robot's stage since this is the hardest part of the game.
