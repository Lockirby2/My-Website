Timeman on Easy isn't just an easier version of Hard Timeman.  You can't memorize his pattern on Easy because you'll dislodge him from his pattern when you shoot him.  This video by DuckswanTheAlbatross explains how to defeat Easy Timeman consistently.  I theorized this strategy might work based off my experience trapping Timeman near the edge of the screen with Gutsman, but when I tried with Bombman I failed to pull it off and wrongly concluded that it didn't work.  DuckswanTheAlbatross proved me wrong though!  It likely requires some practice to do this consistently with Bombman, but it's still easily better than the alternative.
