This is a tool aiding players to manipulate the sequence of battles they will fight during the Imperial Air Force segment in Final Fantasy VI.  The idea is to identify the values of the bytes that FF6 uses to determine which battles you will fight.  You can then determine what values you want them to be in order to get the sequence of battles you want.  Finally, you can determine what actions to take to manipulate those values to the correct ones.  This manipulation can be used to make the IAF segment less luck-reliant or obtain the minimum/maximum amount of EXP.  A detailed explanation can be found in the video.
