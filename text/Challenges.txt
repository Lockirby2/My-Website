This is a catalogue of all the major video game challenge runs that I've recorded over the years.  Challenge runs are the bread and butter of my YouTube channel.  It's fun solving games in ways that they were never intended to be played, and you learn a lot more about the games you play when you do so.  There are many ways to achieve mastery in a game, but I tend to prefer competing against myself to competing against others.  I like to solve the puzzles that a challenge offers from first priniciples and my own game knowledge.  That's not to say that I'll never watch others play and try to emulate them, but when I do a challenge, I want to do something that's at the edge of my comfort zone and be the one to figure out how to overcome my own barriers.  When you compete against others, you inevitably end up following or responding to metagame trends, and that's just not as enjoyable to me.  I also tend to like challenges that <i>end</i> more than something like a speedrun where you can never truly be finished, though I'm willing to dabble.
