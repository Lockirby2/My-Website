As usual, Tifa is our character of choice against Rude, who doesn't use his signature move (Grand Spark) against her.  After wiping his goons, our first order of business is to Steal the Ziedrich.  During this process, Rude will only attack us physically.  Like the last Turk battle, we're only taking minor damage (and not even every turn), so there's not much chance of dying.  Still, the process takes a while since we only have a 1/21 chance of getting it.  Barrier is worth it this time to avoid the fight dragging out longer.
