This boss is a giant pile of HP and little else.  Despite the attack animations, most of its moves are physical attacks that range from weak to pathetic.  It can use them twice per turn, and it'll do so more often as the fight goes on.  At the start, we can chuck Dragon Scales at it regularly, but the fight slows down near the end.  The caveat to using Dragon Scales is that we can't kill the Jamar Armour, as it gives way too much EXP (more than the boss itself, in fact).  Since Dragon Scales damage both parts, we need to periodically heal the Jamar Armour with X-Potions to prevent it from dying.  The targeting in this game can be a bit wonky sometimes, so pressing Select to bring up the name of the enemy we're targeting will prevent any mistakes.

After it loses enough HP, Proud Clod will start taking a couple turns to charge its Beam Cannon attack.  Even though it's magical, it's still shoot-element, so its damage is pretty sad for something that the boss takes two turns to charge.

The main gimmick here is Materia Jammer, a move that will inflict Reflect on us, preventing us from setting up Barrier.  The fastest way to get around this is to cast Debarrier on ourselves to remove the Reflect.  This works quite well near the start of the fight, but later on the boss will start using Materia Jammer more often and spending many turns on Beam Cannon, so Barrier probably wasn't worth the two-turn setup at that point.  Still, the boss is easy, so we're not going to die here even if it could have been taken out a minute or two faster.
