Once again, we'll be using the tried-and-true Poison strat.  FF7 has an incredibly long streak of "Poison and wait" bosses, but we're near the end of them now.  I didn't aim for the 7777 Poison speedrun strat (where the boss will take 7777 damage from Poison when it has 7777 HP) since I didn't know of a good way to set it up here.  It absorbs Fire, so we can't use Molotovs here, and Grenades aren't worth using next to those beefy Poison ticks.  Since we're using Poison, the second phase of the fight is irrelevant once again, so we're only concerned about the first.

Materia Keeper may open the fight with Trine, but we have the Bolt Ring to nullify that and no other accessories worth using here.  Its attacks are all physical, and most of its attacks are all effectively the same.  H. Combo, deals about twice as much damage as the rest though, so it's the one to watch out for.  Defending is our friend here, as we can't do much else and it'll reduce the odds of a critical hit killing us.  Once again, we wait to see if it misses or crits its attacks before deciding what to do with our turns.

This is another boss that felt insurmountable as a kid.  Poison really does work wonders in this game.
