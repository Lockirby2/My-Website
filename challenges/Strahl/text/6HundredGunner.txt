As a kid, I felt like this battle was almost unfairly difficult.  As an adult, I now understand the power of Grenades.  Hundred Gunner is relatively tanky (no pun intended), but with Sadness it's weak enough that the largest chance of a game over is the player suffering a cardiac arrest halfway through the fight.  I meant to heal before Heli Gunner, but overestimated how many Grenades it would survive.

Heli Gunner's first phase is the most dangerous in the fight, as it can inflict Poison and Sleep.  Luckily, we don't need to wait for our ATB to refill after we wake up in this game, so it's unlikely to stunlock us with Sleep.  Healing at ~100 HP seems pretty safe.  Heli Gunner's second phase has the most raw damage, but is still less dangerous.  IDK why I bothered with Healing Wind here, but this is another fight with a long cutscene beforehand, so I didn't want to clean up the mistakes.

I decided to shove Rufus in this video since he's barely a boss and there's no save point between the fights.  I didn't realize that killing Dark Nation gave EXP for another couple of hours, but it's only 70 EXP, so it doesn't cost us much of our EXP headroom.
