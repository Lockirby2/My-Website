This boss is a nice break after the previous.  On paper, her regular attacks are stronger than JENOVA Life since she deals more damage and can attack three times per turn, but resisting her only element (Fire) makes her less dangerous.  The main difference is the lack of a big attack like Aqualung, removing any tension from this fight.  She instead uses Silence.  Oh no!  Anyways, we just need to attack and heal here.  It's finally not cheesy to use our heavy hitting items like Dragon Scales, which do relatively mediocre damage against her high Magic Defense anyways.

Cait Sith is used once again because Schizo wasn't the last boss we need him at a high level for.  If you're wondering why we're using Cait in particular for the tough bosses, it's simply because he has a high HP pool.  For any set of ten levels (e.g., 20-30), the expected HP growth increases linearly between two points that are manually defined for each character.  While Barret gets the best HP at high levels (90-99), Cait gets the highest HP in the level 20-30 range that's all-important for LLGs.  The high HP pool also makes this boss easier, as a bonus.  While Cait being ahead of the level curve is nice for now, we'll need to stop feeding him EXP eventually to avoid breaking the Low Level clause.
