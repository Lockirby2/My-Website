While in the Shinra Building, we can win the most important Materia in the challenge, the Elemental Materia, from Mayor Dominic. With this, we can put Elemental-Fire in Aerith's armour.  Rolling Fire is easily the strongest attack we've seen until this point, but with Fire resistance it isn't notable anymore.
