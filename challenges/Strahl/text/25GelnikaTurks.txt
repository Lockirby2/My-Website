As a kid, I thought that this fight was an enormous difficulty spike that must have been intended to be fought later since they hit so hard.  As an adult, I have no idea what I was thinking.  As soon as Rude casts a spell, his AI breaks and he'll never take another turn.  Reno's Neo Turk Light inflicts Confuse, but we can protect against that with the Peace Ring.  So after surviving the first couple turns, we only need to deal with the weak Short Staff attack, which is only used half the time.  We don't even bother setting up Barrier.
