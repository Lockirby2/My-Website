Here's her attack pattern:

Stop (if she has MP)
Nothing
Nothing
1-3 Lasers
1-2 Lasers
1-3 Lasers
Gas (1/3 chance)

She can start the fight at a couple places in the pattern, but it makes no difference.  Stop can last until the second set of Lasers, which means that we can die if she hits us with five Lasers in a row before it wears off.  Luckily, Defend in this game is a priority action.  If we input Defend before the Stop animation ends, it'll go off even though we shouldn't be able to act.  This means we'll never die because of Stop.  She'll run out of MP for Stop after a whopping three casts, which just gives us a free turn after that point.  In this attempt, Stop just missed three times, which made the fight a bit faster.

After that, we mostly just keep a watch on our HP.  Everything does ~65 damage, so we can only survive four hits.  As long as we have enough HP to survive whatever's coming on the next turn, we're good.  That said, it's important to play this fight on Wait mode.  When JENOVA chains multiple Lasers together on a single turn, the amount of time between Lasers can vary a lot.  In fact, it varies enough that she could straight up take her next turn before we can confirm that she's not going to fire another Laser.  Using Wait mode allows us to ensure we act after her turn is over.

I got cheeky at the end and used Defend to survive W-Laser chains when I otherwise didn't have the HP, but this was silly because I forgot that she could have just killed me with Gas before I could heal.  Each of those mistakes caused a 1/9 chance of death and didn't even save a turn.

Despite appearances, Gas can't poison us.  It probably should poison TBH.  It doesn't seem like she can crit us either (I took a lot of hits over my attempts and never got crit once), which is great.
