Tifa is used here because Rude will never use spells on her and he'll hesitate sometimes instead of attacking.  We target Rude here because we only need to kill one Turk and Rude will heal Reno once his HP starts getting low.  We mostly just need to keep our HP above ~230 to survive the worst combination of attacks.  Reno's Turk Light hurts, but his Short Staff may as well be a wet noodle at this point.  This means that we may take 230 damage or 20, and it's just random what happens.

Our damage options are nothing new, just Poison and Molotovs.  You can definitely make a mistake on Active mode because the targeting in this game depends on where an enemy is on screen when you move the cursor.  So if Rude runs out to attack you, you may misinput.  Other than that caveat, this battle is short and free.
