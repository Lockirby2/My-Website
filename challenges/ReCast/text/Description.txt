The final battle is actually the hardest fight in this playthrough IMO.  You'd think that would be obvious, but that isn't usually the case TBH.  While we could easily prepare to make any of the four fights reliable, preparing for all four fights at once requires a bit more thought.  In particular, tier 2 and Kefka want different setups, but we have to make do.

The first tier is mostly physical attacks and ST magic attacks, so it isn't too bad.  We can enter the fight with status effects, so we enter with float to deal with Magnitude 8 and vanish to help dodge physical attacks.  These won't stick around forever, but they make the fight faster and easier regardless.  We take out the head first because it is the one that we can kill the fastest (Terra can hit a weakness) and it can Glare Daryll to remove her Berserk.  Then we take out the Long Arm because it can spam Shock Wave.  Terra is specced to survive a physical attack and reheal with Tek skills, Locke can reapply his vanish if he loses it, Daryll is almost physically invincible (with the exception of Glare), and Celes' Evade is 107, so she'll dodge around five in six attacks.  Shock Wave also can't harm Celes or Daryll.

At the end of the tier, we make sure that we're at full HP since we need as much HP as possible to survive Tiger's Flare Star.  We also manipulate the ATB so that it's impossible for Short Arm to slip in a kill against Celes just before we finish him off.

Tier two is the hardest part of the battle.  Magic is mostly disabled by Runic, but he can use Garnet to pierce it and kill off a character.  Tools can use both Diffuser and Absolute Zero, and Tiger can use S. Cross and Flare Star.  Even Hit is threatening because he can punch Celes and kill her, leaving us open to Magic's assault.  In the fights I did, this happened far more often than the odds would dictate.

Celes needs to set up Runic as fast as she can.  She can only die to Hit or Garnet.  Daryll is safe from nearly everything thanks to the Paladin Shield's elemental protections and perfect defense, but she can be affected by status effects and Garnet.  Terra can tank spells or a punch from Hit easily, so she's as tanky as Daryll.

Locke opens this phase with a Thunder Shield equipped because Tools can use Diffuser on the first turn and neither Absolute Zero nor S. Cross is possible.  We swap out his shield to a Frost Shield immediately to protect against Absolute Zero because Diffuser will now be Runiced.  We prioritize this over the Flame Shield because it's more dangerous if Tools kills Locke.  Why?  Well, Flare Star's damage is divided among all the party members it hits.  If Locke is dead when Flare Star is used, it will hit the remaining characters harder.  Flare Star also does damage based on the party's level, but it does so by choosing a character's level at random.  If somebody is dead, and Daryll is chosen by Flare Star, we lose the fight.  In any other scenario, we're probably okay.  So we really don't want Tools (or anything else) to kill a character right before Tiger attacks.  There's only so much that we can do to prevent this though.

Tools is the obvious first target, even though Tiger is the most dangerous.  It has the lowest HP by a wide margin and we can hit its weakness with BoltTek.  It's also worth killing because it can use Missile to confuse somebody or kill Locke if Celes has already died.  Once Tools is dead, we can equip the Flame Shield on Locke to defend against S. Cross.  It's also lucky that Daryll hit Magic for 9999 damage because it knocks him into his second phase where he can't use Garnet anymore.

Tiger is next because he's the real threat in this tier.  Once he's dead, Hit and Magic have no way to kill us.  We finish off Magic last since Hit's 10-Hit Combo could certainly kill Celes, and we don't want her dead when the tiers transition because we'll lose her permanently.

Tier 3 is a break.  In this battle I learned that Daryll's berserk is re-applied when switching between tiers even if she already lost it.  Girl is quite easy to kill because she's actually weak to a whole pile of elements now, though unfortunately her level is quite low.  The Meteo spells that Sleep used in vanilla when at low HP have been turned into Shock Wave, which isn't nearly as dangerous.  They really don't have any threatening attacks now, assuming Runic is up.  We can also prevent Sleep's insta-killing death counter by summoning Fenrir.  I did make a mistake here since I was supposed to kill off Locke before the end of this phase so that Biggs could take his place against Kefka.  This makes the Kefka fight a bit scarier than it was supposed to be.

Kefka's Goner is genuinely threatening here.  Only a character with very high magic defense can survive it.  Terra and Biggs were meant to be the survivors, and they'd be able to get the party back on their feet in a turn without much chance of dying.  With only Terra, this gets a lot riskier.  With a Force Shield, Terra has perfect magic defense, so she doesn't take any damage from Goner whatsoever.  The real danger is in the turns after.  If Terra is hit with Bad Breath or some Havoc Wings slip through my high Evade, this fight could easily be lost.

I also made a second mistake; I just let Daryll attack instead of removing her berserk and debilitating Kefka.  Berserk makes the first phase of the fight faster; this is useless because Kefka is not dangerous in his first phase.  Debilitating Kefka would have made the second phase of the fight faster, which could have been the difference between winning and losing.

On the first Goner, we don't waste our Phoenix summon because we still have image status.  I forgot that Terra was muted on the second Goner, so we weren't able to cast Phoenix that time around.  On the third Goner, we finally bust out Phoenix and use it to help make the final push.  Kefka barely goes down before he's able to connect against Terra with Bad Breath.
