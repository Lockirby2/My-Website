The idea behind this challenge is to beat FF6 in as few turns as possible.  Anything selected from the menu counts as a turn, but actions taken by my characters of their own accord do not count.  The latter part may be controversial, but I saw "taking a turn" as something the player does and not something the characters do.  On top of that, I thought it would make for a better video because it doesn't change the turn count much, but enables a couple strategies that I thought were fun.  It also cut down the length of the video, which is nice because I didn't want to edit much more than I did.

Speaking of editing, I'm happy with the editing on this video considering that this was my first attempt at a challenge in this style.  It's more akin to the "new-school" challenge runners that include the entire game in one video and provide commentary than my usual "old-school" approach of showing a boss without commentary and writing a detailed description.  This style seemed more fitting since the big picture of this challenge is more interesting than any of the individual fights.  Everything else is explained in the video.
