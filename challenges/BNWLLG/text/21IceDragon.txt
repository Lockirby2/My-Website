I actually planned on fighting this guy slightly later, but I accidently ran into him on the way up to Umaro, and I just thought, "You know, let's do this, right here, right now." My equipment setup is what I used in the "no HFB's" Tritoch battle I didn't upload, so it is borderline random in the context of this fight.  If Celes/Edgar had been given equipment to survive Absolute Zero/Blizzard, this battle would have been even easier, but as it is there's nothing to be gained from being at full HP.  After the first Surge, I was careful about keeping Float on Edgar/Celes, but it turns out that the first Surge was also the last, so that just wasted my time.  However, even with just Setzer living the MT Ice attacks, there's virtually no chance of dying, as he's pretty slow and gives me plenty of opportunity to revive my party.  The only ways I think I could have died were A) a badly timed Ice Claw on Setzer + MT Ice spell immediately, wiping my whole party, or B) my whole party getting frozen somehow through astronomically bad luck, then picked off.  So, yeah, this dragon was essentially thwarted by a single Iceguard.  But the next battle won't be nearly that simple.
