Bonelord:  This guy mostly just uses instant death attacks, which are no more effective against my low levelled team than any other party.  I didn't bother to equip my characters with anything specific to protect them, and I just let this team's relatively high Stamina do the protecting.  I didn't use HFB's because it was too fast (seriously, he got, like, one attack), and this doesn't take long either, especially with Strago's magic power.  I don't know how lucky I got, but this was my first attempt, barring the one where I murdered him with HFB's.

Tiamat/Tiamat Z:  Or as I like to call them, "Meteo" and "More Meteo."  Magic Evasion is the name of the game here, and it's mostly the reason why I brought this party to the cave.  They can all get at least half decent magic evasion, which allows them to dodge Meteo most of the time.  Gogo and Celes, I think, broke 80% magic evasion, which isn't wonderful compared to other hacks/vanilla, but it works well enough here.  This took several attempts, but it went well in the end.  Strago's use of Refract here was a waste of time in hindsight.  Dodging physical attacks wasn't worth his turn.
