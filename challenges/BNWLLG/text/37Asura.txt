The second and middle difficulty of the statues.  She uses attacks of her element more often than Myria, and with no Flare Star, she doesn't have too much up her sleeve.  With Ice protection from the Iceguards and Image, she spends a very large amount of time doing virtually nothing.  The only part of the battle which catches me by surprise is when she uses Tsunami and then Magnitude.  If I knew she had Magnitude, I would have prepared for it, but this was my first attempt, so I didn't know what to expect.  Even Celes alone is almost invincible, and this is the one statue I can Slow, so recovering is a breeze.  I get back on my feet (with Float this time), and thankfully no other issues crop up, so this becomes an HFB beatdown.
