This guy is pretty much the same fight as in vanilla, except for some minor differences.  Locke dual-wielding in the front can just barely take a Drill, while Celes spends the time Runicing.  If Locke dies, you'll need some serious luck to recover, but Locke has more evasion in this hack, so he's unlikely to be killed by a Drill+Regular attack combo.  It happened three times anyways in other attempts (although I actually recovered, once), but I have no right to complain, given some of the good luck I got later.  He does have Tek Laser now, though, which can OHKO Locke.  Here I get pretty lucky, as Locke barely misses at all, Tunnelarmor misses when I need him to, and most importantly, I never saw Tek Laser.  If Celes gets under 100HP, I heal her, as Tek Laser does about 90 damage to her.  Between Tek Laser and his increased HP, this is slightly more luck-reliant than the original, although if I had done Sabin's scenario first like I should have, I could have given Locke the Atlas Armlet and Knight's Cape, which would have helped.
