FF6 is the game I've been doing challenges in the longest, and it's the most fun to run for me.  Bosses in FF6 tend to have exactly the right mix of randomness and predicatability in their AI scripts.  If AI scripts are too static, then you only need to account for a single sequence of events in your planning, and you win once you have a counter to everything.  If AI scripts are too random, you're just praying that the boss doesn't use that one move that wipes your party.  FF6 strikes the perfect balance where you're accounting for combinations of enemy attacks, you can maintain control over a fight without it being immediately obvious how, and there's some genuine tension even when there's a low chance of a reset.  On top of that, FF6 is one of the easiest games to jump into; there isn't a lot of missable items, points of no return, or EXP management.  This means that you can jump into a playthrough and figure things out as you go along without being concerned that a mistake now will ruin you after another twenty hours of gameplay.  Finally, the game has a lot of mechanics with potential that's untapped because they're overshadowed by others, so there's room for improvisation and creativity.  Even ignoring the parts of the game that aren't related to challenge runs like the story and music, it's easy to play this game over and over again and it's no wonder that it's a classic.
