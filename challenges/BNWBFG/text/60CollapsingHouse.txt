A short but sweet segment. This part contains the very last timed segment in the game. In a way, it's certainly a relief to have that out of the way. Overall, while the WoR's dungeons have gimmicks like the WoB ones do, there seems to be less overall, and the ones that are there make less of a difference (stuff like multi-party dungeons don't hit me with that much of an impact).

As for this segment, it's not quite as easy as the last timed segment, but still easier than all the others. Having only one character is always a huge bonus, and as long as she has enough magic power she can down everything in a hit with Ice 2. Navigation is relatively easy here too, with lots of doors and chests around. And finally, the timer isn't really strict to begin with. I didn't make any mistakes here, so I had plenty of time left.

After that I do some quick shopping. It's almost good that I needed to, as this segment would have been extremely short otherwise.
