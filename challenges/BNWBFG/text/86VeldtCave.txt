After going through perhaps the least straightforward dungeon in the game, I'm suddenly going through perhaps the most straightforward.

The navigation isn't super kind (and indeed, I do get lost once), but it's just normal walking with no gimmicks and a reasonable array of noises to tell me where I am. The battles are fairly straightforward as well. Since Gogo is placed with three mages, using Chakra works out very well here, allowing me to spam costly spells as much as I want. Fire 3 and Raze destroy Grizzlies, Meteor works nicely for the battle with 2x Basilisks and 2x Clucks (although I never end up using it against that fight once here), and Flare will instantly kill a Cluck. Manticores are the most dangerous thing here since they can get nasty if they Berserk all of my Mages with Anger. However, Flare, Break and Black Omen take them down quickly enough that it's unlikely to become an issue.
