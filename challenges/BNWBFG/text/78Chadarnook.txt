I think people expected Chadarnook's gimmick to be more of a thing. And in vanilla, it is to some extent. But in BNW, Chadarnook is a great deal more predictable in that he'll only swap out on his turn. So an easy way to ensure that I never attack the wrong Chadarnook is to only attack right after the boss attacks. I mess this up once during this fight, but overall this strat is easy and dependable.

Since this was my first true attempt at the boss, it was a little sloppy, and this leads to Locke eventually running out of MP. My plan was simply to kill him before Locke ran out of MP, but you know what they say about plans... Until Locke runs out of MP, he can cover my healing needs fairly easily with Cure 2 (Cure 3 is usually a waste). Most of Chadarnook's attacks do low enough damage that an MT Cure 2 from Locke is all I need to throw out. Fenrir is supposed to protect my fighters from Demon Kiss for a while, but this didn't work out too well for poor Cyan here since I got him murdered by attacking the wrong Chadarnook. However, once Locke runs out of MP, things get pretty sketchy because that is pretty much my only source of healing with Setzer/Cyan on pure offense. This means that my only tactic is to switch to zerg rush mode at this point.

Gau is a bit of a fourth wheel here. I don't want him Raging because he'll hit the wrong Chadarnook. This leaves his only purpose to be casting Fenrir and then running utility with Phoenix Downs and Regen spells. This isn't Gau's strongest showing, to be sure.
