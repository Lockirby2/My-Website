I was a little worried about this segment, but in the end I got lucky and it was pretty much just Onion Kids for days. I find a way to take a break halfway through that is a lifesaver as well (the second half before Ifrit/Shiva will be in the next segment). This segment was definitely the scariest dungeon yet at the time, but at this point I really consider it more of a segway into increased dungeon difficulty than actually difficult.

At this point, you can tell that I've really started to get used to actually walking around maps. That used to be the hardest part, but it is making a transition at this point into an afterthought. It's funny that the segment where I felt the battles begin to transition into being halfway difficult is also the segment when I start to notice that maneuvering through maps is become more mechanical and easy.
