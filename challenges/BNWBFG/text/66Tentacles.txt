As for this episode:

This dungeon is fairly long, but the enemies around here are a difficulty valley, so to speak.  The randoms can barely hurt me at all in comparison to the amount of healing that the Life Bells can put out.  Fighting battles is pretty easy around here too.  AuraBolt and 2x Rising Suns slaughter everything around here with no deviations in strategy except for the occasional recovery from Imp status.

The tentacles are pretty easy too.  Reflect Rings and Golem mean that they really only have the Tentacle attack and Seize.  These are unlikely to overwhelm my defenses, so as long as I kill some tentacles before my Golem disappears they can't do much to take me down.
