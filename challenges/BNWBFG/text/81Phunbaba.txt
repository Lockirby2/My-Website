It's been a while since a segment has been such a comedy of errors. As I even predicted beforehand, since this was my first attempt, things were a little "sloppy". But it was even more of a mess than I expected.

The first part is a joke, since this boss is really weak. Avalanche is absorbed by two characters (thanks to the power of Rage) and halved by Strago. His physical attacks are virtually meaningless as well. 1000 Needles is the only threat since it will off a character, but it's not too bad because he doesn't use it very often and doesn't do much to threaten me during the recovery. He can also be Muted, so Bolt 3 isn't amounting to much either.

There's only one reason why this fight is on my radar at all, and that's the switch between his phases. He'll blow away two characters at random, meaning that I don't even know who is in my party! Other than Terra, anyways. On top of that, the Magic menu is organized based on what spells the characters in your party know, meaning I don't know where all my spells are either. Part of the reason why I was alright with two characters Raging Gargoyle was because having only one healer would be a mistake. If they were blown away, I wouldn't have any healing left. But having an excess of healing meant something would come through for me. In my BFLLG I left two characters on the floor (any dead characters will automatically get blown away by BabaBreath) so that I could guarantee the right party for the second half, but I decided against it here.

And of course it ended up being a complete disaster. If the boss itself was threatening at all, I would have died for sure...
