This boss is no joke, especially since I hardly have the optimal party for him. Stamina Sabin is mediocre here, since his healing from Mantra is bad right at the times that I actually need healing (i.e., right after Meteor or Quasar). This is part of the reason why I really don't like Sabin. His other purpose here is to keep Relm going in terms of MP. Unlike against the IAF, Setzer's inconsistency is a pain here. The Brown Dragon tends to either use attacks that are entirely worthless, or attacks that are very dangerous, with little in between. That means that I either want consistent attacks or consistent healing. At least he can use Cure 2 if things get really dire. Relm performs well as always. Her Flares are my main source of damage, and her RegenX is quite useful too.

The worst thing is that the only character I have with Float is Mog. I guess it's better than having no Float casters, but Mog is a pain to keep alive here. While managing my magic defense (and Shell) and HP boosting headgear appropriately keeps Relm, Sabin, and Setzer alive through both Quasar and Meteor, nothing can save Mog from either thanks to his relatively low level. And every time Mog dies, I have to hope that the dragon does diddly-squat for a turn while Mog puts Float back on. I have Mog take care of Poisoning the dragon (the turns he wasted recovering from Poison status saved my butt a couple times), and casting Float, but he's terrible for damage and healing here. Turns out that took up most of his time anyways. On the off turns I had him Defend in hopes of surviving Meteor (fat lot of good that did).

Most of the rest of the dragon's script is irrelevant. Glare can be a pain, and Lode Stone can apply Seizure, but his earth attacks are usually just a waste of time.

Thankfully I didn't make a lot of mistakes during this fight. Things went neither smoothly nor terribly overall.
