Scribblenauts is a game about creating whatever you want with Maxwell's magic notepad to solve puzzles.  This is a game that's most fun if you're trying to mess around and find silly or weird solutions to the puzzles presented, and I tried to show that off in this series as much as possible.  This game has a glitch where writing certain words (e.g., "living room", "bedroom") that spawn multiple objects doesn't increment the item counter at all, but I didn't know about the glitch when I made the series.  Regardless, you could define the rules to be that anything typed into the notepad is considered an item rather than using the in-game item count.

Unfortunately, this is another series filmed on DS with a camcorder.  At least this one didn't require holding the DS like the KSSU series, so I fixed the camera and the DS in place while recording.  It was literally painful to film while playing in this position, but at least it made the resulting quality slightly less bad.
