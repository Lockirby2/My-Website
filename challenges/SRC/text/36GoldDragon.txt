Unless you have Reflect up, the Gold Dragon only uses Bolt-element attacks.  And since Umaro's unique Rage Ring Relic nullifies Bolt, it's a shoe-in to use him here.  The Blizzard Orb gives us more damage than trying to beef up Umaro's weak physical attacks.

As one of the three statues, you'd expect Doom to be a major fight, but she's always one of the most disappointing bosses. Doom also has a gimmick where she'll nullify elements, but with Ultima being non-elemental it's a non-issue.  It really just prevents us from using the Io Rage with Gogo here.
