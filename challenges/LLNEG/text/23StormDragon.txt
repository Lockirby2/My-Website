Considering status immunities work both ways in this game (stopping you from applying or removing statuses), this gives me permanent Vanish statuses.  Now his physicals cannot hurt me either.  This leaves only two threats.

Chadarnook:  With Reflect on, there's not much dangerous that he/she can do at first.  They can kill me physically, but that's about it.  However, after about three Flare Stars or so, Chadarnook starts using Flash Rain.  A lot.  I get pretty lucky not seeing one.  At this point, Chadarnook seemed to hide a lot, coming out for me to hit him and running away again.  This wasted a lot of his turns, already limited by Slow.  That's probably what saved me.  I started running when Girl was around so that none of my Ragers would attack her and waste their turns (Phantasm wasn't really a danger).  At the end, I could have mimiced and used another Flare Star, so I ended well into the clear.  His automatic weakness to Fire was really helpful, as it would have wasted valuable Reflect time to debilitate him before going into the second phase.
