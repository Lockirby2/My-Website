This battle easily required the most restarts so far.

Defensively, this battle is a nightmare.  I need Vanish to withstand the multitude of physicals, and the type of Seize that afflicts Slow.  Golem wears off in less time than it takes to electrocute a pickle and nobody has enough MP to cast Fenrir.  This means that Poison and Bio instantly kill whomever they hit, and Entwine might as well kill them. Luckily, I believe the last Tentacle has nothing that can defeat Vanish, so I'm safe after I kill the other three.

First of all, I instant deathed (death is now a verb) the first Tentacle with Chainsaw because it is too difficult to keep up with its attacks.  Poison builds in damage, so it becomes effective after a while; the hard part is lasting that long.

After the other two are dead, I clumsily defend myself and kill the last Tentacle with the best attacks that I can muster.  I use the Debilitator because 5/8 elements it can penetrate will be useful for something (Fire 2, Ice 2, Bolt 2, Bio, AuraBolt is holy elemental) but my luck is terrible and it debilitates the wrong three first.  However, I can't really complain because the opposite happened at a more important time fairly recently :)  Forgetting Doom for Celes and Vanish for the non-Edgars made this battle a little bit more more difficult and/or luck reliant, but wouldn't have made too large a difference.
