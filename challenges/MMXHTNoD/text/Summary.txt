This is a no damage run of Mega Man X Hard Type, a hack developed by Hart-Hunt, one of my online friends.  This is a difficulty hack that takes every aspect of MMX and makes it tougher.  It's a solid play if you're into difficulty hacks (albeit not <i>that</i> challenging), but I can't say I have a deeper analysis than that because it's been too long since I've played it.

I wanted to feel like I truly mastered this game, as there were a few places during my casual playthrough where I never figured out a satisfactory solution to obstacles.  Sometimes I didn't know how to solve the obstacle and just facetanked a hit to proceed.  This sort of thing always irritates me, so I wanted to "correct" my mistakes by finding ways to beat everything flawlessly.  To me, the fun part was discovering and executing these solutions, and I was never interested in a full-game damageless playthrough.

Overall, the stages wouldn't have been too hard but for my insistence on moving as fast as possible through them.  The difficulty of any given boss varied wildly.  Some of them took an hour or two to beat, while others were nearly free.  I don't really feel like I learned every detail of the boss AI like I did in Mega Man Powered Up.  Against some bosses (e.g., Launch Octopus), I mostly just reacted on intuition and hoped I could react to what they did.  Maybe I could have done better than that... or maybe it's just too random.  I don't have enough memory at this point.  I enjoyed this challenge though.  Each piece of the game was a bite-sized chunk that I could refine without getting too overwhelmed.  Bosses like Launch Octopus and D-Rex were intense too.
