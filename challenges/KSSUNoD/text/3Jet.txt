Jet is a powerful ability with an even more powerful grab attack which can hit more than once.  The only hard part is making sure you don't start moving and then stop Kirby right inside a boss.  Releasing a shock wave is more powerful than crashing straight through a boss so I do this as much as possible.

Boss:                Difficulty:  Description:

Whispy Woods: 1/10    -    Charge, crash, charge, crash...  If he drops apples this is even easier.

LoLoLo/LaLaLa:  2/10 - Other than making sure you don't use a grab attack and hit something on the floor above, you're good.  A grab attack has the potential to kill one of them and half kill the other all in one go.

Mini-Bosses:  3/10 - It's all too possible that one of them can hit you while you're charging if you're not careful.  Some of them have undeniable agility (At least, once they decide to attack)

Kracko:  1/10 - He's pretty much dead before he can attack.

Kracko Jr:  1/10 - Like father like son...

DeDeDe:  2/10 - He spits out lots of easy stars to hit him with, is slow enough to give time to charge, and if you mess up and get cornered, you can just blast straight through him.

Wham Bam Jewel:  2/10 - The long time it takes before you can hit her again makes no difference to jet.  You can even hit her with grab attacks (if she actually lasts that long).

Galacta knight:  4/10 - You have to be very careful (and occasionally lucky), as he can easily hit you while you're charging.  His minions are no issue though and he's easy to hit.  One small mistake can ruin your run though.

Marx Soul:  3/10 - Jet has no easy way to dodge his quick ascending attack or paintball attack, so you need some luck.  Also, if you use a grab attack he may appear directly on top of you.  However, despite my mistakes here, he still goes down fairly quickly.
