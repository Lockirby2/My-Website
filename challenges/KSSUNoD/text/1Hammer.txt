Hammer is the first ability I'm going to use because it is ridiculously powerful.  Not much else to say here.

Anyway, for a breakdown of the bosses:

Kracko Jr:  2/10 - I never lost to Kracko Jr in this challenge.  I found a method that destroys him rather quickly, but he moves fast enough that he might hit me if I make a mistake.

Mini Bosses:  1/10 - Considering none of them really get to attack, the only danger usually comes from me running into them (yes, that actually happened once).

Kracko:  1/10 - Kracko is too predictable.  He really can't hit me unless I make a really stupid mistake.

Whispy:  1/10 - How could anybody lose to Whispy?  With Hammer?

Kabula:  1/10 - Piece of cake.  I've fought this guy too many times to be tripped up by him.  I won't bother rating him in the future, as he's always the same.

LoLoLo/LaLaLa:  1/10 - Sometimes these two can take a while, but it's hard to actually get hit.

DeDeDe:  3/10 - Really doesn't deserver his rating.  The majority of my deaths came from him, and almost every death I had fighting him was caused by me falling on his head AFTER his hammer spin move.  I blame the awkward position I have to sit in to film the game.

Wham Bam Jewel:  1/10 - Only lives long enough to use four attacks that I can see coming anyways.

Galacta Knight:  3/10 - He has several dangerous attacks, but he'll often use a jump attack (though he didn't here), after which is perfect to hit him with a fire hammer.  His minions are honestly his most dangerous feature, but they also provoke a fire hammer.  His laser attacks also allows much plowing.

Marx Soul:  2/10 - If he spawns in the wrong place too much, he may get enough attacks to become less predictable.  However, his vast HP usually falls quite quickly.

My camera:  5/10 - For making me restart more times than any individual boss.
