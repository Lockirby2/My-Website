In this segment I go through the Dark Palace (less accurately named than in Link Between Worlds) to get some key items.

I show off my new Quake Medallion by hitting some enemies with it, and I show off my Rupees by giving them to a monkey.

This is the first dungeon where I planned a route beforehand to save time (and make it easier).

The Goriyas are pretty non-threatening because you can control them.  Seriously, why would anything attack you like this?

For the most part, this dungeon makes enemies easy to deal with because you have plenty of room to maneuver around them.  You can't kill the turtles yet, but they move slowly anyhow, and won't avoid walls trying to get to you.
