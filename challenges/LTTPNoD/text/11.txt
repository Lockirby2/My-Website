This boss is a really fun fight.  This goes fastest if you're willing to go right next to the boss and shoot arrows rapidly (arrows can only be shot when the last one hits something, so if you're right next to what you're shooting you can hit stuff quickly).  I stay further away to make sure that I avoid damage though.  In my circumstance, using the Ice Rod may have been faster here, in hindsight.  I make sure to stay in line with the Lamolas either horizontally or vertically, since their shots only go on diagonals at first, and my arrows only go on the compass directions.  Once two are dead, the third will start shooting rocks in eight directions, so arrows become a dangerous move.  My best move at this point is to spin slash the last Lamola when it comes out, then just hit it normally.  The spin slash has to be carefully placed lest you bounce into a rock, but it's not too hard.
