After a Chirstmas hiatus, Link goes on to claim the magic of Ether.  Ether is essentially a full screen wipe (for a decent MP cost), which makes it very useful.  It is by far the most useful of the full screen wipe medallions (for reasons I will mention later), and I actually start to abuse its power less as I go along, as it gets more broken.  But for the next dungeon, Hyrule Castle, I hope you like Ether animations.
