Here I finally get back into a dungeon!

I don't use bombs to kill things often, but I do use one the first room.  It's funny, because another person on YouTube (bitbucketcynic) who ran this game no damage did the same thing, completely independently of me.  Great minds think alike?

Oh man, that second room caused its fair share of restarts.  All sorts of things flying at you here, and everything somehow manages to be in the wrong place at the wrong time.  Originally I tried walking through that narrow patch of land to avoid the water blobs, but it's so close to the fireball shooter that this actually made it far more difficult than it had to be.  I eventually decided that walking as far as possible from that thing was for the best.

Most of the dungeon is fairly neutered.  The water spiders don't move too quickly, the fish don't even spawn by the time you get out of the room half the time and many of the enemies can be killed in a straightforward manner.

Ether makes in extremely easy to deal with the room at the top of the dungeon.  I just have to block one fireball and move through it as fast as possible, and I won't get hit.  Grabbing that 20 Rupee chest was honestly rather pointless.

I see no reason to grab the Big Chest in this segment, as I'm going right by there anyways in the next.
