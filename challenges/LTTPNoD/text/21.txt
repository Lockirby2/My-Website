In this segment, I grab a heart piece and the Quake medallion, as well as enough Rupees to pay off the monkey in the next segment.  For the most part, Quake is the weakest of the medallions, since it only hits enemies on the ground as opposed to all enemies onscreen, and it turns them into bags of...evil?...instead of killing them outright.  It also doesn't freeze them like Ether, which would be useful too.  However, this medallion does have one use (besides the necessity of using it to enter Turtle Rock).
