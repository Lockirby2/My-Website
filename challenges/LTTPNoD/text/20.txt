In this segment, I Ether my way up Aghanim's (not guaranteed to be the actual spelling) Tower.

The ball and chain guys are pretty easy to simply peg between the eyes with arrows.

Now for more limited visibility rooms.  I don't like fighting in these rooms because there aren't many landmarks here to remind me which enemies come from where.  This makes it difficult to remember how to deal with each floor, or to react and figure it out on the spot.  Ether is very useful in this dungeon, but it does cost quite a bit of MP.  With a Potion in my back pocket though, that's not too big an issue.

The room with the hole is easy without Ether, so I don't waste my MP.

Because I get an MP refill from a red spear thrower, I go nuts for the rest of the dungeon, even in the easy pot room.

Aghanim is fairly simple here; just stand horizontally displaced from him and spin slash.  That way, if he fires a blue shot you'll be at an arm's length from it, and it won't hit you when it explodes.
