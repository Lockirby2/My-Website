I finally go and beat that boss that everybody was mentioning to be difficult on the forums!

I would have actually done this with the Fire Rod alone, and beat the whole dungeon without the sword, but there's a curtain in the way which can't be cut away without the sword.  So that's how it is.  I didn't think of warping back to the Light World at the end of my last segment, and I didn't think of using the flute at the beginning of this one.  This meant that I had to take the long way around to Kakariko.

I let the Blue Bouncy Crab hit the spike for some lulz.  I then grab a key, which looks like it's supposed to be used on the door right next to it.  Weirdly enough, that room also requires a large detour to get to, and is also entirely useless.  Instead I keep the key and use it to skip the next one.

The first room can be skipped because I have the key already, and the next can be skipped because that puzzle is easy to break.  Another room gets toasted with Ether, and I cut my way through that blasted curtain.

Time for the boss.  I spent about 15 minutes practicing with a save state just before the boss, and I won 5 or 6 out of about thirty attempts that I had during that time.  Boy was I surprised when I went for the real attempt and beat the whole thing first shot!

This boss is somewhat of a luckfest, but you can increase your chances by staying towards the centre of the screen, since there are regular blocks on the side there and you have extra room to maneuver.  There is a lot of stuff to keep track of already, so I don't try to pay attention to the floor, and just hope that it moves me favorably.  I hit the boss with my Fire Rod when it's far away, and my sword when close up, as it's easier to hit with in close quarters.  Killing the boss quickly is a great asset, and this was actually my second fastest battle out of all the practice runs that I took (a whole 8 seconds long).
