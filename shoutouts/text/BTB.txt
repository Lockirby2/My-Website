BTB and I are a bit of a strange pair.  Superficially, we don't seem like people who would naturally get along.  He's just about the most vulgar person I know, while I don't even go as far as swearing.  But beneath the exterior, BTB has a deep kindness in him.  If you're going to go to somebody for a deep, heartfelt discussion, he's one of the best people you could talk to.  He's also one of the creators of Final Fantasy 6 Brave New World, which I consider to be the best FF6 hack.  While he would typically offload much of the credit to his co-creator Synchisi and the community who have continued to build on top of Brave New World, BTB is the foundation of the pinpoint-accurate game balancing that Brave New World is known for.
