The gimmick behind this boss is that it will run away from you.  You have to follow her around the maze and catch up to her three times.  This isn't too hard since she'll walk on the path you need to follow to catch up to her.  Once you reach her the final time, you'll enter the "real" fight, where she's incapable of hurting you.  I wasn't sure at first if she'd do anything, so I hedged my bets by having Rory use MP on a Fire spell, but when I saw an attack come in for 0 damage I mashed Attack for the rest of the fight.  With my flagging item stock, it was a relief to see a freebee boss here.

Sylph led me pretty close to the next boss, so I found it fairly quickly.
