This is another status-heavy boss, but it's less "all-in" on statuses than the last one.  This is also the first fight where I took advantage of stacking a couple Guards and a couple Power-ups at the start of the fight, which made her physical attacks wimpy.  This boss can inflict slow and poison, which should be countered by Haste and Remedy respectively.  I was a bit lax with doing this though.  Raw damage isn't a concern here.  At one point I was distracted and failed to notice that Fire did lousy damage, so I ended up using it again.

The main threat is that she'll eventually use Swallow to eliminate one character from the fight.  The character's HP doesn't decrease, thankfully, so we don't need to waste resources rehealing.  However, we need to deal enough damage to take the KO before she eats everybody.  She didn't attempt to use Swallow for quite a while, so I suspect Lamia is designed not to eat people right away.  By the time she started to chow down, I figured I had already dealt enough damage to close out the fight, so I didn't rush it too much.  That said, when Ellis got poisoned, I really should have given him a Potion rather than using Remedy.  Having more MP to exploit the Ice weakness would have made the end of the fight less risky, though she died on the next hit anyways, so my prediction was good enough.

I wasn't sure where to go after that, so I started wandering in the door maze again.  I managed to stumble upon the next boss and the entrance to the next floor.
