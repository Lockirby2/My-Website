I was still getting my bearings at this point, so this was easily the clumsiest fight in the game.  At that start of the fight, I felt like there was a lot of stuff happening at once, and rather than using Wait mode to my advantage, I started flailing around and inputting attack commands.  This would normally work fine for the first battle of an RPG, but in a game with only ten bosses, I figured that every boss would count.  After Rory died, I adjusted my mindset and threw up some buffs and the Venom status.  Since it was mostly attacking with Ice moves, I skipped out on Guard after reading that it would only help against non-elemental attacks.  Somebody else who reviewed the game called it an "anti-RPG", and somehow that fits right in the fact that non-elemental attacks are the only "element" that you can protect against. :P

I had a bit of trouble reading the font for the damage/HP numbers and enemies like to attack at the same time that your own actions are going off, which led to me having a bit of trouble tracking my HP values early on.  Plus, it still hadn't registered that I should slow down and take my time.  Because of this, I didn't always heal when I should have in the earlygame.  Luckily, this is the least punishing part of the game.  Perhaps I should have restarted after this fight, but I was still holding out hope for a resetless playthrough at this point and didn't think that a subpar first battle would make the rest of the game impossible, so I pushed on.

After exploring the rest of the maze, I found the next boss.  I grabbed what I figured was the last chest so that I wouldn't forget it.  You're given a few Bombs on the first floor, but I made a conscious decision not to use them yet.  Since Bombs are advertised as dealing damage to multiple targets, this implies the existence of at least one fight with multiple targets and that said fight is the best time to use them.  I let them stew in my inventory for now.
