This boss is similar to the previous, but spends more time using physical attacks.  Multiple Guards would have been great here if I had known to stack them.  Fishing for a hit on Rory to maximize Potion healing meant that Ellis never got Haste, but it didn't end up being a big deal.  I assumed it had high magic defense after using Fire, but I should have experimented with Ice to see if it was just a Fire resistance.  At the end of the fight, the worm started buffing with Power-up multiple times, which could have been scary if the fight went on any longer.  Once it started buffing, I figured that I didn't have much time before things went off the rails, so I pushed damage and killed it ASAP.

I may have gotten lucky with the order of its attacks here.  If it could have randomly used Power-up at any point, then an early Power-up stack could have made this brutal on resources.  If it only uses Power-up as a desperado, then the clean win was more skill than luck.  Also, this boss is what finally reminded me that Guard and Power-up stack, so I started making better use of these spells from here on out.  Either way, this boss didn't consume many resources.
