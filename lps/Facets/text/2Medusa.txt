From this fight onwards, I set up buffs immediately to maximize their usefulness before going to town.  Haste is easily the most important buff, as extra turns are helpful against everything, so I always think it's worth the large MP investment.  I can always feel the difference when Haste isn't up.  Unfortunately, it also takes three turns to set up on everybody, so it's best for Rory to give it to himself first so that he can put it on the other two faster.  Guard is also great, as it can make you invincible to non-elemental attacks when stacked enough.  Power up is best at improving damage when setup at the start, and it's good to estimate how many you'll want before you start attacking.  You get diminishing returns and spend a lot more MP if you use it too many times.

This is probably the most straightforward boss in the game.  She uses Bolt spells, physical attacks, and some "Gaze" attack that never connected.  Guard is nice to prevent her Double Attack from dealing hefty burst damage.  I probably should have used a second Guard to gut her physical damage, but I forgot that it stacked in the earlygame.  It turns out that she's vulnerable to fire and ice damage, but I was having trouble telling if my spells were dealing ~30 damage or ~90 damage, so I prioritized Claire's turns too much when she should have been the one throwing Potions (I maybe should have skipped Power-up as well).  As for the Potion management, I generally tried to wait until a character got hit to throw a Potion so that it would heal HP as well as MP and maximize the benefit.  This strategy was effective in the earlygame, but less great later.  Despite the minor mistakes, I was still happy with this fight overall.

After this, I returned to the boss sitting out in the open to challenge her before moving on.
