Here is a catalogue of some of the Let's Plays and longplays that I've done.  I'm not going to post every LP I've ever made to this site.  Many of my LP videos are slow-paced and the commentary isn't amazing, so I don't think they should all be posted here.  I will only post the playthroughs that I feel are genuinely good content, whether because I took the time to edit them into something nice or because the LP style fits the video I created.

I don't consider myself great at commentary.  I'm not much of a conversationalist IRL, so I have difficulty filling time in an engaging way unless there's actually something important to talk about.  It would work alright if I edited down the videos, but I historically haven't done that either because it takes such a long time for just an LP video.  On top of that, when I play a game, my mind is typically focused on the game itself, and this means that I need to actively remind myself to say things.  For these reasons, I tend to prefer producing polished, heavily edited content over LPs.  I may try to improve at this in the future though.
