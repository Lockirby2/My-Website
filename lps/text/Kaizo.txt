This is a playthrough of Kaizo Mario World without using an infinite lives patch or any save states.  I added tips and tricks to the video itself to guide people who are looking to beat the game themselves.

Kaizo Mario World is a game that I played for the first time a long time ago.  I hadn't had any other experience with Kaizo hacks, as the Kaizo scene was less developed back then and I wasn't even aware of any other options.  I wasn't able to complete the entire game back then because I hit a brick wall at Ultra Star.  I started getting frustrated, felt it was eating too much of my time, and wasn't confident that I could do it.

Fast-forward about a decade, and I decided to give it another shot.  I had gotten a lot better at SMW since then and more persistent to boot.  This time around, the story was different, and I was able to complete the hack in its entirety.  It was still the hardest SMW hack I had beaten at that point, but it was cathartic to get "revenge" on the game that had stumped me so long ago.
