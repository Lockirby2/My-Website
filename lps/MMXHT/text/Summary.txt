This is a playthrough of Mega Man X Hard Type, a hack developed by Hart-Hunt, one of my online friends.  This is a difficulty hack that takes every aspect of MMX and makes it tougher.  It's a solid play if you're into difficulty hacks (albeit not <i>that</i> challenging), but I can't say I have a deeper analysis than that because it's been too long since I've played it.  I put effort into editing this one, so this series is a decent watch if you want to chill and watch me play some Mega Man casually.
