Out of all the maverick fights that I've beaten IRL so far, this one has been my favorite! It took a while, but once I began to pick up his pattern I felt as though I was able to take him on. It's tough, but not luck-reliant or unfair at all!

As for the stage, it was well designed as well. I had to bother learning a little more about the enemies to deal with Hard Type (in vanilla, it was often easy to clear everything from the screen before it even mattered), but it was pretty reasonable once I knew what to do. I like having the dash boots right away; it opens up more options for stage design without necessarily forcing the player to go to Chill Penguin's stage first.

Enemy damage seems reasonable in this stage. It's enough to keep me on my toes, but not enough to remove all hope of a safety net.
